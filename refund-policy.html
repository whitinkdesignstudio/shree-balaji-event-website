Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains how Shree Balaji Event LLP ("the Company") handles cancellations and refunds for bookings made for our event management services.

1. Booking advance

A booking is confirmed only after receipt of the agreed advance payment. This advance secures the date, vendors, and resources allocated to your event and is adjusted against the final invoice.

2. Cancellation by the client

If you wish to cancel a confirmed booking, please notify us in writing as early as possible. Refund of amounts paid will depend on how far in advance the cancellation is made, since vendor bookings, venue holds, and preparatory work are committed on your behalf ahead of the event date:

  • Cancellations made more than 30 days before the event date: advance amount refundable after deducting expenses already committed to vendors/venues on your behalf.
  • Cancellations made between 15–30 days before the event date: a partial refund may be made, after deducting committed vendor costs and a cancellation charge.
  • Cancellations made less than 15 days before the event date: the advance amount is generally non-refundable, as most vendor and venue commitments are final at this stage.

3. Cancellation or changes by the Company

In the rare event that we are unable to deliver a confirmed service due to circumstances within our control, we will offer a full refund of amounts paid for the affected service, or propose a suitable alternative in consultation with you.

4. Circumstances beyond our control

Where an event cannot proceed due to circumstances beyond either party's reasonable control (such as government restrictions, natural calamities, or venue unavailability for reasons outside our control), we will work with you to reschedule the event where possible; refunds in such cases will be limited to amounts not already committed to third-party vendors.

5. Refund method and timeline

Approved refunds will be processed to the original mode of payment within 7–10 business days of approval, subject to the processing timelines of the payment gateway and the client's bank.

6. How to request a refund or cancellation

To request a cancellation or refund, please contact us with your booking details through our Contact Us page.
