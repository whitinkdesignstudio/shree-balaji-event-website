Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12/9/2026

Shree Balaji Event LLP ("the Company", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us while enquiring about or engaging our event management services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, billing and shipping/venue address, event details, and payment information when you enquire about, book, or pay for our services.

2. How we use your information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, plan and execute events, process payments, issue invoices, and communicate updates related to your event. We may also use contact details to share offers or updates, which you can opt out of at any time.

3. Payment information

Online payments made through our website are processed by our payment gateway partner (Razorpay). We do not store your card, UPI, or net banking credentials on our own servers — these are handled directly by the payment gateway in accordance with its own security standards.

4. Sharing of information

We may share necessary event details with third-party vendors (venues, caterers, decorators, and other suppliers) strictly for the purpose of executing your event. We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain personal and event information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, and as required to comply with our legal and accounting obligations.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us by contacting us through the details on our Contact Us page.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out through our Contact Us page.
